Identity chip for people. Takes last-two initials of `name` and picks a stable gradient hue from the name, so a person's avatar color never changes.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Nguyễn Văn An" size={32} />
<Avatar name="Trần Thị Mai" size={40} />
```

Rounded-square (`--radius-md`), not a circle. Use `size` 24–48 in tables/cards, 40+ in profiles.
